# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, be kind, and assume good intent. Technical disagreement is welcome.
Harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, and discriminatory language are not.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space
operated by Weaion Labs.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to:

```text
hello@weaionlabs.com
```

Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may remove comments, close threads,
or ban accounts when needed.
